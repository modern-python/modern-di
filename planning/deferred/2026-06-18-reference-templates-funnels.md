# Reference templates as funnels

Frameworks and blog posts link to **starters**, not to libraries. A template is
the artifact a host framework's documentation will actually point at.

## Why it is open

The org publishes `fastapi-sqlalchemy-template` and `litestar-sqlalchemy-template`.
There is **no FastStream template and no Typer template** — and FastStream is half
the intended beachhead (see
[`beachhead-framework-default`](2026-06-18-beachhead-framework-default.md)).

So the funnel is missing for exactly one of the two frameworks the adoption
strategy targets.

## Revisit trigger

Alongside the beachhead outreach — the template is the thing the beachhead
framework's docs would link to, so it needs to exist before the ask, not after.
