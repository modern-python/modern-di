# Reference templates as funnels

Frameworks and blog posts link to **starters**, not to libraries. A template is
the artifact a host framework's documentation will actually point at.

## Why it is open

The org publishes `fastapi-sqlalchemy-template` and `litestar-sqlalchemy-template`.
There is **no FastStream template and no Typer template**, so two of the
integrations with official support have no starter to link.

## Revisit trigger

Before asking any framework's docs to link to `modern-di` — the template is the
thing they would actually link to, so it needs to exist before the ask.
