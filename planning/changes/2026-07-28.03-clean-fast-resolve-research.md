# Design: Clean-and-fast resolve research

## Summary

A research effort, not a code change. Enumerate every unit of work the resolve
path pays **per node**, put the same three questions to each, prototype the
candidates that answer well, measure them, and land one ranked report at
`planning/audits/2026-07-28-clean-fast-resolve-report.md`. Prototypes live on a
throwaway spike branch and are discarded; surviving findings become their own
change bundles afterwards. Every stance in `decisions/` and `deferred.md` is
reopened for this effort by maintainer ruling — which raises the evidence bar
rather than lowering it, since each was closed with a measurement.

## Motivation

The resolve path currently buys speed with duplication, on purpose:
[2026-07-19.08](2026-07-19.08-perf-readability-audit.md) records the inlined
override-guard / navigate / closed-check preamble as "copy-pasted across all six
compiled closures on purpose to hold the per-node frame at 1". Today that costs:

- the 6-line override front-guard, written **six** times in `resolver_compiler.py`;
- `navigate + reopen-if-closed`, written **four** times;
- the `CreatorCallError` routing, written **four** times;
- the positional/kwargs fork, doubling both the transient and the cached body;
- three private-attribute reach-throughs with `SLF001` suppressions, each
  justified in a comment by nanoseconds — `registry._resolvers.get` inside
  `container.py:225`, `cache_registry._items.get` inside
  `resolver_compiler.py:227`, and `target._prepare()` in four closures.

The prior sweep concluded "there is no hot-path work to do here", but it
deliberately shipped **no measurements** — its perf findings were bench-mapped
hypotheses by scope ruling. That is the gap this effort closes.

The framing worth testing is that the two axes are not opposed but share a
cause: work is duplicated *because* it is per-node, so a concern moved to
compile time deletes nanoseconds and copies together. Two candidates are already
visible in the source and motivate the sweep:

- **Override front-guard → compile time.** `overrides_registry` is the root's,
  shared tree-wide by every child (`container.py:135-141`); resolvers are
  memoized on the equally tree-wide `providers_registry`. So
  `override()`/`reset_override()` could clear the resolver memo — the same
  invalidate-on-mutation mechanism [2026-07-18.02](2026-07-18.02-dispatch-floor-invalidate-on-mutation.md)
  already licensed — and an overridden provider would compile to a
  constant-returning closure. Deletes the guard from six closures and two
  `LOAD_ATTR`s plus a branch from every node of every resolve.
- **Closed-check hoist.** `if target.closed: target._prepare()` sits in four
  closures, but the container handed to any resolver is already open:
  `resolve_provider` reopens the entry container, and the only way a resolver
  reaches a *different* container is `_navigate`. The check belongs inside
  `_navigate` alone — four copies and a per-node attribute load plus branch,
  gone, with no new rule.

## Design

### Inventory

Enumerated from source, with today's copy count:

| Concern | Site | Copies |
|---|---|---|
| Override front-guard | all six compiled closures | 6 |
| Scope navigate (int compare + `_navigate`) | 4 closures | 4 |
| Closed-check + `_prepare()` | 4 closures | 4 |
| Cache-item fetch (inlined `_items.get`) | cached closure | 1 |
| Cache sentinel check | cached closure | 1 |
| Args build (positional / kwargs fork) | transient + cached | 4 bodies |
| Creator call + `CreatorCallError` routing | transient ×2, cached, `Factory._call_creator` | 4 |
| Error-prepend `try` | every build site | 5 |
| Entry dispatch (closed-check, inlined `_resolvers.get`, `RecursionError` wrap) | `container.py:215-230` | 1, per top-level resolve |

### Screen

Three questions per concern:

1. **Movable?** Decidable at compile time, or hoistable behind an invalidation
   trigger, rather than evaluated per resolve.
2. **Does moving it delete copies?** Counted.
3. **What rule does moving it add?** Named — a new invalidation trigger, a new
   compile-time variant, a widened contract.

Routing: yes/yes/nothing-new is a do-first candidate. Yes/yes/one-new-rule is a
maintainer call. No/yes is clean-only and lives under the trade budget below.
Yes/no is pure perf and must clear the existing `~2x-or-nothing` bar from
[`2026-07-19-child-lazy-alloc-declined`](../decisions/2026-07-19-child-lazy-alloc-declined.md).

**Trade budget.** A clean-only change may cost up to ~3% on its scenario — the
run-to-run CV band already documented — and must state the cost. Above 3% it
becomes a needs-decision item rather than a silent drop, so that
merge-the-four-bodies-into-one style proposals survive long enough to be judged.

### Measurement

The guard tier is the wrong instrument here. `benchmarks/README.md` records that
a guard median is quantized to one `perf_counter` tick (41 ns on an M4, 23% of
G2's ~180 ns) and that this has already produced one false reading of a real
change. Most candidates move 10-30 ns.

Primary instrument is therefore the direct harness that README already
prescribes for judging a small change — `timeit` at 200k iterations, median of 9
— driven by one script living on the spike branch only.

- **Interleaved A/B/A**, one process, one session. If the two baselines disagree
  by more than the reported spread, the run is discarded.
- **Guard IDs for orientation only** (G1/G2/G3/G5/G8/G9/G12/G16); the number
  comes from the direct harness on that scenario's graph shape.
- **Cold cost measured, not assumed.** Anything adding recompilation — the
  override candidate above all — is measured on G8's cold build-and-first-resolve
  and on an override-churn shape modelling a test suite (N overrides interleaved
  with M resolves), since that is where `modern-di-pytest` users would feel it.
- **Faithfulness gate.** A prototype's number is quotable only if `just test`
  passes on the spike at that commit; a prototype that drops an error path always
  looks fast. Coverage and lint are waived on the spike, the test suite is not.
- **Free-threaded** check only for candidates that change shared-state access,
  and only as "does not regress" — non-scaling is already diagnosed as CPython
  refcount contention.
- **Comparative tier once, at the end, finalists only** (`just bench-report`).
  This effort records what would move; it does not edit
  `docs/introduction/performance.md`.

Cleanliness is captured in the same commit as the number: copies deleted, `noqa`
suppressions removed by code, hot-path source lines, per-node frame count
verified with `dis` rather than by eye, and the count of new rules introduced.

### Report

One row per inventoried concern, survivor or not, carrying: site (`file:line`);
the three screen answers; measured perf delta with method, spread and scenario
(or `screened out` plus reason); the countable cleanliness proxies, with
before/after source for anything reaching do-first or needs-decision; the
`decisions/`/`deferred.md` item it collides with, quoted, and whether its
evidence meets that item's own revisit trigger; and a bucket — do-first,
needs-decision, cleanup, skip, screened-out.

The reopening of settled stances is what makes the collision field load-bearing:
a finding may contradict a ruling, but only by carrying the evidence that ruling
asked for.

## Non-goals

- **No code ships from this effort.** The spike branch is discarded; survivors
  become their own change bundles under normal review.
- **No published-performance edit.** `docs/introduction/performance.md` and the
  comparative table stay as they are.
- **Nothing outside per-node resolve work.** With every stance reopened, the
  inventory table is the scope boundary; anything else is footnoted, not chased.
- **No free-threaded scaling work** — diagnosed as CPython's, per `deferred.md`.

## Testing

Not a code change, so no suite gate. Landed correctly means: the report exists
at `planning/audits/2026-07-28-clean-fast-resolve-report.md`; every inventory row
is present with a bucket; every quoted number names its method, spread and
scenario and was produced on a spike commit where `just test` passed; every
finding that collides with a `decisions/` or `deferred.md` item quotes it and
addresses its revisit trigger; `file:line` citations spot-check clean (3-5); and
`just check-planning` and `just check-links` pass with this change file present.

## Risk

- **Measurement error read as signal** (likelihood high without care, impact
  high — it would corrupt a maintainer ruling). Mitigated by the direct harness
  over guard medians, A/B/A interleaving, and reporting spread; the README's
  one-tick precedent is the standing warning.
- **Unfaithful prototypes.** A spike that skips an error path measures fast and
  is wrong. Mitigated by the passing-`just test` gate on any quoted number.
- **Scope creep from the blanket reopening.** Mitigated by the inventory as the
  boundary and by the collision field, which prices a reopening in evidence.
- **A good number for a bad rule.** The override candidate can be fast and still
  be wrong: it couples the resolver memo to override state, `close_sync` resets
  overrides at the root, and `architecture/concurrency.md` currently describes
  override as unlocked and unguarded. It is filed needs-decision regardless of
  its number.
